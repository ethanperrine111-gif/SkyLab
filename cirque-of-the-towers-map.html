Permits & Rules

No permit required for overnight travel in Bridger Wilderness; free self-registration at the trailhead.

Group size max 10 people. Camp 200 ft from lakes, streams, and trails. Bear canisters strongly recommended; grizzlies documented in the range. No campfires above 9,200 ft.

Getting There

Big Sandy Trailhead is 45 mi east of Boulder, WY. From Pinedale, take US-191 south, then 20+ mi of dirt roads (Lander Cutoff / Big Sandy Opening). High-clearance recommended after rain. No cell service past Boulder.

Weather & Season

Snow lingers on Washakie Pass and Lizard Head Plateau into mid-July. Prime window is late July through mid-September. Afternoon thunderstorms are near-daily — be off the passes by noon.

Nights routinely drop into the 20s °F. Mosquitoes brutal through late July.

Water & Filtering

Reliable water at every named lake and most creeks. The driest stretch is the Lizard Head Plateau — top off at Bear Ears Jct. and again before climbing.

Treat everything; giardia and livestock (sheep bands in some drainages) are both present.

Bailouts

Two shortcuts if the weather turns:

  • Skip Washakie Pass — reverse to Big Sandy Lake and go direct over Jackass Pass (18 mi round trip).
  • From Lonesome Lake, exit via Jackass Pass any time; it's the shortest way back to the trailhead.

Bring

  • Topo: Beartooth Publishing Southern Wind River Range or USGS Big Sandy Opening / Lizard Head Peak quads.
  • Bear canister · trekking poles · microspikes if pre-August.
